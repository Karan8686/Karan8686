### 👨‍💻 About Me

- 📱 **Mobile & Full-Stack Engineer** with deep expertise in **Flutter & Dart**, cross-platform engineering, and **TypeScript/React full-stack** systems.
- 🚀 Lead Architect & Developer of [**`CV Bucket`**](https://github.com/usesinspiration-ship-it/Cv_bucket) — Engineered an end-to-end resume indexing & search platform utilizing React 19, Express, Cloudflare R2 (S3), and Firestore fuzzy search.
- 🪨 Creator of [**`caveman-plus`**](https://github.com/Karan8686/caveman-plus) — A lightweight, zero-dependency programmatic text compression engine for LLMs and AI workflows that cuts 40–70% of tokens while preserving technical precision.
- 📦 Open-source author of modular Flutter packages including [**`adaptive_image_loader`**](https://github.com/Karan8686/adaptive_image_loader) and [**`adaptive_image_picker`**](https://github.com/Karan8686/adaptive_image_picker).
- 🎯 Focused on clean architecture, reactive state management, high-performance APIs, and intuitive developer tooling.
- 💬 Let's talk about **Flutter, Full-Stack Architecture, TypeScript, Cloudflare, or AI Workflows**.
